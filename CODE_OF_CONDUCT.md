# Seagalia code of conduct

This code of conduct applies to Vigie and to every other project of Seagalia.

Seagalia and its members, contributors, sponsors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Seagalia community a positive, inclusive, and safe experience for everyone. Seagalia welcomes contributions from everyone who shares its goals and wants to take part in a healthy, constructive, and professional way.

This code of conduct supports a community where everyone feels safe to take part, to bring new ideas, and to inspire others. That includes everyone, whatever their ability, age, background, body size or type, caste, disability (visible or invisible), education, ethnicity, family status, gender, gender identity and expression, geographic location, level of experience, marital status, nationality or national origin, native language, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, socio-economic status, or any other dimension of diversity.

These guidelines let every member of the community work together. This document lists both the expected behaviour and the behaviour the community doesn't tolerate. It isn't an exhaustive list of what you must or mustn't do, so read it in the spirit in which it's written.

## Expected behaviour

Seagalia expects its members, contributors, and leaders to:

- Take part in the community actively and authentically.
- Keep the commitments they make, and tell others as soon as possible when something prevents them from keeping one.
- Try collaboration before conflict, and respect differing opinions, styles, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Disagree in a professional and respectful way.
- Show empathy and kindness, and choose their words with care.
- Look out for the people around them, especially from a position of influence. Alert the moderators when they notice a dangerous situation, someone in distress, or a breach of this code of conduct, even one that seems minor.
- Accept responsibility for their mistakes and apologize to the people affected, whether the harm was intended or not. When someone says they were harmed, listen, make amends and change the behaviour.
- Focus on what is best for the whole community, share knowledge, and make sure no single voice dominates a conversation.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following behaviours are unacceptable in the Seagalia community, online or offline, in private or in public:

- Violent language or threats against another person.
- Sexist, racist, or otherwise discriminatory jokes and language.
- Deliberate intimidation, following, or stalking, in any setting.
- Personal insults, especially those related to gender, sexual orientation, race, religion, or disability, including misgendering, name calling, and mockery.
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language, jokes or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Bullying, tone-policing, or repeatedly dominating a conversation.
- Publishing or threatening to publish someone's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately revealing any private aspect of a person's identity without their consent ("doxing"), except where needed to protect vulnerable people from intentional abuse.
- Excessive or unnecessary profanity.
- Knowingly making harmful false claims about a person.
- Marketing to community members without their express approval.
- Repeatedly contacting a community member who asked to be left alone.
- Sustained disruption of community events, meetings, or presentations.
- Recording or photography at community meetings and events without explicit permission.
- Advocating for, or encouraging, any of these behaviours.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Consequences of unacceptable behaviour

Everyone must stop unacceptable behaviour as soon as they're asked to.

Any community member or moderator who sees unacceptable behaviour should report it as described in [Report an incident](#report-an-incident). The moderators investigate each report to assess its severity and its impact on the community. Depending on the risk and impact, they may respond with one of these actions:

- **Correction.** A private, written warning that explains the violation, why the behaviour was inappropriate and what behaviour is expected from now on. A public apology may be requested.
- **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with the people involved is allowed for a set period, in community spaces and on external channels such as social media. Breaking these terms may lead to a ban.
- **Temporary ban.** No interaction or public communication with the community for a set period. Coming back usually requires a meeting with a moderator.
- **Permanent ban.** No public interaction with the community at all.

The moderators decide which action to take. Participants must comply at once, and further action may follow when they don't.

Every community member is entitled to one appeal, made the same way as a report. The requested action stays in force while the appeal is considered, and the decision on the appeal is final.

## How reports are handled

The moderators review and investigate every report promptly and fairly. A moderator with a conflict of interest steps aside from the case.

The moderators keep an internal record of every incident, and they respect the privacy and safety of the person who reported it. When a public statement is needed, the identities of the people harmed and of the reporters stay confidential unless they ask otherwise.

If you think you were unfairly accused of breaking these guidelines, follow the same process as for a report.

## Where this code of conduct applies

These guidelines apply to every member of the Seagalia community and to every Seagalia activity, including:

- Issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions in the Seagalia repositories.
- Representing Seagalia at public events and on social media.
- Seagalia meetings and events, in any setting.
- Communication between community members about Seagalia, in any medium.

Actions taken outside Seagalia spaces can still harm the health of the community when they concern Seagalia or its members. The moderators may consider such actions when they have a demonstrated impact on community members.
